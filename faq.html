Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about COSPA pools, programs, and registration

The season runs approximately 80 days each summer, from Father's Day weekend to Labour Day. In 2026, the first pools open June 8 (Bowview, Forest Lawn, Highwood, Silver Springs) and the remaining pools open June 13 (Millican Ogden, Mount Pleasant, South Calgary, Stanley Park). Most pools close September 1, with Bowview, South Calgary, and Mount Pleasant offering an extended season until September 20.

Activities become viewable on Amilia on April 11, 2026. Registration opens to the public on April 14, 2026 at 9:00 AM.

Check your pool's Amilia store for the most up-to-date schedule, or call the pool directly. Pool phone numbers are listed on our Our Pools page.

Public swim is $6 per person. Children 1 and under swim free.

All COSPA pools are heated to 27 degrees Celsius.

COSPA uses the Lifesaving Society Swim for Life program for all swimming lessons. We transitioned from Red Cross to Lifesaving Society in 2022.

The Lifesaving Society provides a transition chart for Red Cross swimmers. View the Red Cross transition chart.

The Lifesaving Society provides a transition chart for YMCA swimmers. View the YMCA transition chart.

The Lifesaving Society provides a transition chart for I Can Swim participants. View the I Can Swim transition chart.

Yes. Families with a City of Calgary Recreation Fee Assistance card receive 50% off public swim walk-in admission.

Public swim admissions and swim lesson registrations are not refundable or transferable. If a swim is cancelled by staff before the scheduled start time, a credit will be issued that is valid until the end of the season. If a swim is evacuated after it has started, no refund or credit is issued.
