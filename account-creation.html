Create Your Amilia Account

Set up your account before April 14 so you're ready to register the moment programs go live.

1

Visit your pool's Amilia store

Choose your neighbourhood pool from the Our Pools page and click the Register Now link to go to that pool's Amilia store.

2

Create an Amilia account

Click "Sign Up" or "Create Account" to start your registration. You'll need a valid email address.

3

Enter your information

Fill in your name, contact details, and address. This is the primary account holder information.

4

Add family members

Add each family member who will be participating in programs. Include their name and date of birth so they can be placed in the correct lesson level.

5

Browse available programs

Activities become viewable on April 11, 2026. Browse swim lessons, public swim, lane swim, Aquafit, and more.

6

Add programs to your cart

Select the programs and time slots that work for your family. Registration opens to the public on April 14, 2026 at 9:00 AM.

7

Complete payment

Review your cart and complete payment. You'll receive a confirmation email with your registration details.

Your account works at every COSPA pool

Your Amilia account transfers between pools — no need to create a new account for each location. Set it up once and register at any of our 8 pools.

Need help with your account? We're happy to assist.

Email info@cospa.ca

Calgary Outdoor Swimming Pools Association manages 8 outdoor pools across Calgary on behalf of the City of Calgary.
