Admission Policies

Pricing, weather, cancellation, and refund policies for COSPA pools

Public Swim Pricing

$6 per person

Children 1 and under swim free.

Fees for other programs (swim lessons, lane swim, Aquafit, rentals) are set by each pool. Check your pool's Amilia store for current pricing.

Weather Policy

Public swims proceed through rain and cool temperatures. We're outdoor pools in Calgary — a little weather is part of the experience.

Swims will be cancelled in the event of lightning, hail, or heavy smoke. Safety of swimmers and staff always comes first.

Cancellation & Refunds — Public Swim

Public swim admissions are not refundable or transferable.

Staff cancellation before start time: If a swim is cancelled by staff before the scheduled start time, a credit will be issued. Credits are valid until the end of the current season.

Evacuation mid-swim: If a swim is evacuated after it has started, no refund or credit will be issued.

Swim Lessons

Lesson fees are paid at the time of booking through Amilia.

Swim lesson registrations are not refundable or transferable.

Calgary Outdoor Swimming Pools Association manages 8 outdoor pools across Calgary on behalf of the City of Calgary.
