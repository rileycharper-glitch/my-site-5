Who We Are

The Calgary Outdoor Swimming Pools Association (COSPA) manages 8 outdoor pools across Calgary on behalf of the City of Calgary. Our pools are community gathering places where Calgarians of all ages come to swim, learn, and connect every summer.

COSPA offers swimming lessons through the Lifesaving Society Swim for Life program, public swim, lane swim, Aquafit, and private rentals. Through our partnership with Vecova, we also provide adaptive aquatics programming at Forest Lawn and South Calgary outdoor pools.

Our season runs approximately 80 days each summer, from Father's Day weekend to Labour Day, with select pools offering an extended season into late September.
